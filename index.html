Why sourcing gets difficult

Finding a manufacturer shouldn't be the hardest part of building a fashion brand.

Referrals, scattered information, unclear MOQs and a dozen conversations before you know who can actually make your product. The challenge isn't finding a factory. It's finding one that fits your product, quantity and production needs.

Traditional sourcing

  • Manufacturers found through fragmented networks and referrals.
  • High MOQs that rule out smaller production runs.
  • Many supplier conversations and handoffs to manage yourself.
  • Little visibility once production starts.

The Voz Cruda way

  • Structured supplier matching based on your product and production needs.
  • Practical options for your quantity, including pooled lots through Smallotz.
  • One point of coordination for clearer communication.
  • A repeatable process that supports reorders and growth.

Smallotz by Voz Cruda

Order what you need. No minimums.

Low MOQ clothing for small brands. Factories set minimum order quantities that small brands can't reach on their own. Smallotz pools your order with other buyers making the same kind of product, so together you reach the factory MOQ and each of you orders only what you need.
