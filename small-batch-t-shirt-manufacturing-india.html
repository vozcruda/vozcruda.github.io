Custom T-Shirts in Small Quantities: A Guide to Low MOQ T-Shirt Manufacturing in India

T-shirts are the most popular first product for new brands. Here is how to choose fabric, GSM, fit and print method, and how to get a small batch made at factory quality.

Short answerTo make custom t-shirts in small quantities in India: choose your fabric and GSM (for example 240 GSM cotton for an oversized fit), pick a print method that suits small runs (DTF or DTG for many colours, screen print for bold few-colour designs), approve a sample, then produce through a low MOQ route. Step 2: Pick the right GSM

Our fabric guide covers GSM for every product.

GSM (grams per square metre) measures fabric weight. Heavier fabric feels more premium and holds structure; lighter fabric is cooler.

  • 160 to 180 GSM: light everyday tees, summer wear, merchandise.
  • 180 to 220 GSM: mid-weight regular-fit tees with a quality feel.
  • 220 to 260+ GSM: heavyweight oversized streetwear tees with a structured drape.

Step 3: Decide the fit

  • Regular: classic fit, works for most customers.
  • Oversized: dropped shoulders, wider body and longer sleeves. Popular in streetwear.
  • Boxy: wide and slightly cropped.
  • Slim: closer to the body, often with some stretch.

Write measurements for every size in a tech pack. Our tech pack guide shows the key points to measure, and the t-shirt tech pack example shows a complete size chart.

What drives t-shirt cost

  • Fabric and GSM: heavier and combed or bio-washed cotton costs more.
  • Quantity: setup costs spread over more pieces means a lower unit price. This is why pooling helps.
  • Colours: custom dyed colours can carry dye-lot minimums.
  • Decoration: number of print colours, print size and placements.
  • Branding: custom neck labels, hang tags and packaging.

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ for custom t-shirts in India?

Many factories ask for 300 to 1,000 pieces per style for fully custom t-shirts, often with a per-colour minimum. Small units accept less at a higher price, and pooled lots on Smallotz let you order smaller quantities at close to bulk pricing.

What GSM is best for an oversized t-shirt?

Oversized streetwear tees are commonly made in 220 to 260 GSM cotton for a heavier, structured drape. Regular-fit everyday tees are often 160 to 200 GSM.

Which printing method is best for small batches?

DTF and DTG are cost-effective for small runs and multi-colour designs because they need no screens. Screen printing gives excellent durability and becomes cheaper per piece as quantity grows, especially for designs with few colours.

What is bio-washed cotton?

Bio-washing is an enzyme treatment that removes loose surface fibres from cotton. It gives the fabric a smoother, softer feel and reduces pilling.

How long does it take to make custom t-shirts?

After sample approval, small batches of custom tees typically take a few weeks, depending on fabric availability, dyeing and printing. Add time for sampling and, with pooled orders, for the lot to fill.
