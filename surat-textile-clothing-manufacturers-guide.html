Surat Clothing and Textile Manufacturers: A Buyer's Guide for Small Brands

Surat is India's best-known centre for synthetic and blended fabrics, sarees and dress materials. For a clothing brand it is as much a fabric source as a garment hub. Here is how to use it well.

Short answerSurat, in Gujarat, is India's best-known hub for synthetic and blended fabrics such as polyester, viscose, georgette, chiffon and crepe, and for sarees, dress materials and embroidered ethnic wear. Clothing brands use it mainly to source fabric and ethnic wear. For cotton knit basics like t-shirts, Tiruppur is the better choice.

Our overview of garment hubs in India gives Surat a short paragraph. This guide is for brands that work with synthetics or ethnic wear and want to understand how the cluster actually works.

How the Surat cluster works

Surat's textile industry is a chain of specialised businesses:

  1. Weaving and knitting units make greige (unfinished) fabric, much of it on power looms.
  2. Processing houses dye, print and finish the fabric.
  3. Embroidery units add machine embroidery, sequins and embellishment.
  4. Traders in the city's textile markets stock and sell finished fabrics, sarees and dress materials.
  5. Garment units stitch ethnic wear, kurtis and other garments.

As a small buyer you will usually deal with a trader or a manufacturer who coordinates the rest. Knowing the chain helps you ask where a delay or quality problem might come from.

Buying fabric in Surat

Many brands that never stitch in Surat still buy fabric there. A few things to get right:

  • Specify precisely: composition, weight or GSM, width, weave or knit, finish and colour. Synthetic fabrics that look alike can differ a lot in quality.
  • Ready vs custom: ready fabrics from traders are available in smaller quantities. Custom colours, prints or weaves usually need larger minimums per design and colour.
  • Test before bulk: check shrinkage, colour fastness, pilling and drape on a swatch or a sample garment.
  • Record the lot: ask for the article or quality number so you can reorder the same fabric.

MOQs in Surat

Minimums depend on what you are buying. Ready fabrics and ready ethnic wear from traders can be bought in modest quantities. Custom-made garments, custom prints and embroidery designs usually come with minimums per design and per colour, and processing houses have batch minimums for dyeing and printing. Sourcing from Surat step by step

  1. Collect references: fabric swatches or garments with the drape, sheen and weight you want.
  2. Shortlist suppliers who specialise in your fabric or product, not general traders who stock everything.
  3. Get swatches and a sample garment, and test them.
  4. Write a tech pack for stitched garments, including embroidery placement and colour references.
  5. Confirm on a written order: fabric quality number, quantity, price, delivery date and payment terms.
  6. Inspect on arrival before cutting or selling.

Is Surat right for your brand?

If your range uses synthetic or blended fabrics, or you make sarees, dress materials or embroidered ethnic wear, Surat should be on your list. Frequently asked questions

What is Surat famous for in textiles?

Surat is known for synthetic and blended fabrics such as polyester, viscose, georgette, chiffon and crepe, and for sarees, dress materials, lehengas and embroidered ethnic wear.

Is Surat good for clothing manufacturing?

Surat is strongest as a source of synthetic fabrics and for sarees, dress materials and ethnic wear. It has garment units too, but many brands buy fabric in Surat and have garments stitched there or in another hub.

Can I buy small quantities of fabric in Surat?

Traders in Surat's textile markets often sell ready fabrics in smaller quantities, while custom weaving, dyeing or printing usually has higher minimums. Ask each trader for the minimum per design and per colour.

Is Surat good for cotton t-shirts?

Not usually. Cotton knitwear such as t-shirts and hoodies is better sourced from Tiruppur. Surat suits synthetic and blended fabrics and ethnic wear.

How do I avoid quality problems when buying from Surat?

Ask for exact composition, GSM or weight and width, get a swatch or sample before bulk, check colour fastness and shrinkage, and confirm everything on a written order. Similar-looking synthetic fabrics can differ a lot in quality.
