Short answerVoz Cruda LLP is an apparel sourcing company in Bengaluru, India. It matches fashion brands, boutiques, D2C sellers and private label businesses with suitable clothing manufacturers and coordinates production. Its service Smallotz lets small buyers order low MOQ clothing by pooling orders with other buyers until the factory's minimum order quantity is reached.

What we do

Finding a factory is rarely the hard part of building a fashion brand. Finding one that fits your product, quantity and production needs is. Voz Cruda works on that problem in four steps:

  1. Share your requirement: product, quantity, budget, specifications and timeline.
  2. Match with suppliers: we find manufacturing options that fit your category, capability and volume.
  3. Choose your path: compare the most relevant options before you commit.
  4. Produce and repeat: we coordinate next steps and help build a process you can reorder from.

Our services are supplier matching, MOQ aggregation (order pooling), production coordination and private label sourcing.

Smallotz: low MOQ clothing by order pooling

Factories set minimum order quantities that small brands often cannot reach on their own. Smallotz pools your order with other buyers making the same kind of product, so together you reach the factory MOQ and each of you orders only what you need. That gives small brands access to factory production runs, keeps less cash locked in stock, and makes it practical to test new styles in small lots and reorder what sells. Read more in What is MOQ aggregation?

Who we work with

We work with apparel businesses that do not have their own sourcing department: emerging fashion brands making their first collection, growing apparel businesses adding manufacturing options, and private label businesses sourcing products under their own brand. On the supply side, we are building a network of manufacturers matched by real product capability.

Where we source in India

We build around the regions where India makes clothes: Tiruppur (knitwear and basics), Surat (synthetic and woven apparel), Jaipur (ethnic wear and fashion), Delhi NCR (fashion and private label) and Bengaluru (contemporary apparel). See our guide to India's apparel manufacturing hubs.

Frequently asked questions

What is Voz Cruda?

Voz Cruda LLP is an apparel sourcing company based in Bengaluru, India. It matches fashion brands and growing apparel businesses with manufacturers that fit their product, quantity and requirements, then helps coordinate the path to production.

What is Smallotz?

Smallotz by Voz Cruda is a low MOQ ordering service for clothing. It pools your order with other buyers making the same kind of product, so together you reach the factory's minimum order quantity and each brand orders only what it needs. It is available at smallotz.vozcruda.com.

How does a pooled lot on Smallotz work?

You pick a product, add the quantity you need, and your order joins a lot with other buyers. Once the pooled lot reaches the factory minimum, production goes ahead and Voz Cruda coordinates it.

What kinds of clothing can I source through Voz Cruda?

T-shirts and tops, shirts, bottomwear, dresses, ethnic wear, outerwear and hoodies, private label products, and custom requirements.

Who does Voz Cruda work with?

Emerging fashion brands, growing apparel businesses, private label businesses, boutiques and D2C sellers, especially those without their own sourcing department. Brands ordering under 300 units are usually pointed to Smallotz.

Which manufacturing regions does Voz Cruda work with?

Voz Cruda builds around India's apparel hubs: Tiruppur for knitwear and basics, Surat for synthetic and woven apparel, Jaipur for ethnic wear and fashion, Delhi NCR for fashion and private label, and Bengaluru for contemporary apparel.

Can garment manufacturers partner with Voz Cruda?

Yes. Manufacturers can share their product categories, capabilities, location, MOQ, capacity and lead times. Voz Cruda matches that capability with buyer requirements, and pooled Smallotz lots can bring orders that meet a factory's MOQ.

How do I contact Voz Cruda?

Email sales@vozcruda.com or send a sourcing request from the form on vozcruda.com. The team replies within 2 business days.
