How to Find Clothing Manufacturers in India: 7 Ways That Work for Small Brands

Finding a manufacturer is not the hard part. Finding one that makes your product, at your quantity, to your standard, is. Seven ways to do it, and the pros and cons of each.

Short answerThe main ways to find clothing manufacturers in India are B2B directories like IndiaMART and TradeIndia, visiting the right hub (such as Tiruppur for knitwear), trade fairs, referrals from other founders, social media, sourcing partners like Voz Cruda, and pooling platforms like Smallotz. Start from your product and quantity, build a shortlist, then verify every option with references and a paid sample.

Before you search: know what you need

Manufacturers respond faster to specific enquiries. Have these ready:

  • Product type and a few reference images
  • Fabric, composition and GSM
  • Quantity per style and colour
  • Sizes and decoration (print, embroidery)
  • Target price and timeline
  • A simple tech pack, if you have one

7 ways to find a manufacturer

1. B2B directories

IndiaMART, TradeIndia and similar directories list thousands of garment suppliers searchable by product and city. They are a fast way to build a long list, but listings mix real factories with traders, so verification is essential.

2. Visit the right hub

Each cluster specialises: Tiruppur for knitwear, Surat for synthetics and ethnic fabrics, Jaipur for prints, Ludhiana for winterwear, Delhi NCR for fashion and private label. Visiting lets you see many units in a few days. See India's apparel hubs.

3. Trade fairs and exhibitions

Textile and apparel fairs bring manufacturers, fabric mills and trim suppliers under one roof. They are useful for meeting many suppliers at once, though many exhibitors target large buyers.

4. Referrals from other founders

Founder communities, WhatsApp groups and industry contacts often point to the most reliable units. Ask what the factory does well, and what went wrong.

5. Social media and LinkedIn

Many manufacturers now post production videos on Instagram and LinkedIn. It is a good way to see real work, but still verify before ordering.

6. Sourcing partners

A sourcing partner like Voz Cruda matches your product and quantity with manufacturers whose capability fits, then coordinates sampling and production through one point of contact. It saves weeks of searching and vetting.

7. Order pooling platforms

If your quantity is below a factory's minimum, pooling platforms combine your order with other buyers. You get access to factories that would otherwise decline your order.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a clothing manufacturer in India for a small brand?

Start from your product and quantity. Use B2B directories and hub visits to build a shortlist, ask for references and samples, or use a sourcing partner like Voz Cruda that matches small brands with suitable manufacturers.

Are IndiaMART suppliers reliable?

B2B directories list many genuine manufacturers alongside traders and middlemen. Treat a listing as a starting point and verify GST details, the factory, references and a sample before ordering.

How do I contact a clothing manufacturer?

Send a short, specific enquiry: product, fabric and GSM, quantity per style, sizes, decoration, target price and timeline, with a tech pack or reference images. Specific enquiries get faster, more accurate replies.

Why do manufacturers not reply to small brands?

Many factories prioritise orders that meet their MOQ. Clear enquiries help, and pooled orders or a sourcing partner can get small brands a response by bringing combined volume.

Should I visit the factory?

If you can, yes. A visit, or at least a live video walk-through, shows real capability, working conditions and quality control far better than photos.
