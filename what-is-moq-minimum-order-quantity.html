What Is MOQ? Minimum Order Quantity Explained for Clothing Brands

Every new brand runs into MOQs in its first week of talking to factories. Here is what the term means, how factories calculate it, how to read an MOQ in a quote, and what to do when yours is lower.

Short answerMOQ stands for minimum order quantity: the smallest order a manufacturer or supplier will accept. In clothing it is usually counted per style, and often per colour, for example "300 pieces per style, 100 per colour." Factories set MOQs so their fixed setup, fabric and printing costs are spread over enough pieces. If you need fewer, you can negotiate, pay a surcharge, or pool your order with other buyers.

"What is your MOQ?" is usually the first question a new brand asks a factory, and the answer often ends the conversation. Understanding where the number comes from makes it far less of a wall. This guide explains the term from scratch. If you already know it and want ways to order small, go straight to our guide to low MOQ clothing manufacturers in India.

MOQ meaning in plain words

A minimum order quantity is a supplier's rule: "we will not take an order smaller than this." It shows up across every industry, from packaging to electronics, but in clothing it has a few quirks:

  • It is usually per style, meaning one design. Ten designs of 30 pieces each do not add up to a 300-piece MOQ.
  • It is often per colour too, because each colour may need its own fabric dye lot.
  • Sizes usually count together. A 300-piece MOQ normally covers the whole size run, though some factories want a minimum per size.
  • Your suppliers have MOQs as well. Fabric mills, dyeing units, print units and label makers each have their own, and the factory's number often reflects theirs.

Why manufacturers set MOQs

Some costs are the same whether a factory makes 50 pieces or 5,000. The MOQ is the point where those costs stop hurting.

Fixed costWhy it does not shrink with your order
Pattern and gradingPatterns for every size are made once per style
Line setupMachines are set and operators briefed for each new style
Fabric and dye lotsMills and dyeing units work in minimum batches
Print or embroidery setupScreens, test prints and embroidery programs are made per design
Custom trimsLabels, tags and zips are sold in minimum lots
Admin and quality checksSampling, paperwork and inspection happen for every order

Below the MOQ, a factory either loses money or charges a price per piece that most brands cannot sell at. That is why the number exists, and why it can move when you remove one of these costs.

How to read an MOQ in a quote

Quotes word MOQs in different ways. Here is how to read the common ones.

Quote saysWhat it usually means
MOQ 500 pcs500 pieces of one style; ask whether colours count separately
MOQ 300 pcs, 100 per colourAt least 300 of the style, with each colour at 100 or more
MOQ 200 pcs per colourwayEvery colour you add needs another 200
MOQ 300 kg fabricA fabric minimum; the number of garments depends on GSM and size
Below MOQ: +X per pieceThe factory accepts a smaller order with a surcharge

When in doubt, reply with your exact plan, such as "one style, two colours, 80 pieces each, S to XL", and ask whether it works. That gets a clearer answer than asking for the MOQ in general.

Typical MOQs in Indian clothing manufacturing

Minimums differ by product, fabric and the size of the factory. As a rough guide, many factories quote a few hundred pieces per style for knit basics such as t-shirts, often more for denim and washed garments, and less for handwork-led ethnic wear. Our low MOQ guide has a table of common ranges by category.

Smaller job-work units and sampling studios often accept far fewer pieces, at a higher price per piece. Export-focused factories usually ask for more.

What to do when your order is below the MOQ

  1. Ask what drives the number. Fabric, print, trims or line setup each have different fixes.
  2. Remove the cost behind it. Use stock fabric and colours, fewer colours, standard trims and simpler prints. See how to negotiate MOQ.
  3. Price in a surcharge if your margin can take it. Check with the clothing cost calculator.
  4. Pool your order. In MOQ aggregation, several brands order the same base product, so the combined lot meets the factory minimum and each brand gets its own quantity.

How to choose the right order quantity

An MOQ tells you what a factory will accept, not what you should buy. For a first drop, start from demand: estimate what you can sell in 6 to 10 weeks, order that, and reorder the styles that sell. Holding less stock keeps cash free for the next drop. Our guide on starting a clothing brand with low investment covers first-drop sizing in more detail.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of MOQ?

MOQ stands for minimum order quantity. It is the smallest number of units a manufacturer or supplier will accept in one order.

What does MOQ mean in clothing manufacturing?

In clothing, MOQ is usually the smallest number of pieces a factory will make of one style, and often of each colour. A quote of 300 pieces MOQ with 100 per colour means at least 300 pieces of the style, with no colour below 100.

Why do manufacturers have MOQs?

Each production run has fixed costs such as pattern grading, machine setup, fabric and dye lot minimums, print screens and custom trims. The MOQ is the quantity at which those costs are spread over enough pieces to make the order worthwhile.

Is MOQ per order or per style?

In apparel it is most often per style, and sometimes per colour or per fabric. Always ask the supplier what the number covers, because it changes how many pieces you really have to buy.

What is a zero MOQ or no MOQ supplier?

A no MOQ supplier accepts any quantity, even a single piece. Print-on-demand services work this way, printing on ready-made blanks. Factories that cut and sew from fabric almost always have some minimum.

Can I order less than the MOQ?

Often, yes. You can negotiate by using stock fabric, fewer colours or standard trims, pay a below-MOQ surcharge, or join a pooled order where other buyers make up the rest of the factory minimum.
