Before you search: know what you need

Manufacturers respond faster to specific enquiries. Have these ready:

  • Product type and a few reference images
  • Fabric, composition and GSM
  • Quantity per style and colour
  • Sizes and decoration (print, embroidery)
  • Target price and timeline
  • A simple tech pack, if you have one

7 ways to find a manufacturer

1. B2B directories

IndiaMART, TradeIndia and similar directories list thousands of garment suppliers searchable by product and city. They are a fast way to build a long list, but listings mix real factories with traders, so verification is essential.

2. Visit the right hub

Each cluster specialises: Tiruppur for knitwear, Surat for synthetics and ethnic fabrics, Jaipur for prints, Ludhiana for winterwear, Delhi NCR for fashion and private label. Visiting lets you see many units in a few days. See India's garment hubs, or our Tiruppur guide for knitwear.

3. Trade fairs and exhibitions

Textile and apparel fairs bring manufacturers, fabric mills and trim suppliers under one roof. They are useful for meeting many suppliers at once, though many exhibitors target large buyers.

4. Referrals from other founders

Founder communities, WhatsApp groups and industry contacts often point to the most reliable units. Ask what the factory does well, and what went wrong.

5. Social media and LinkedIn

Many manufacturers now post production videos on Instagram and LinkedIn. It is a good way to see real work, but still verify before ordering.

6. Sourcing partners

A sourcing partner like Voz Cruda matches your product and quantity with manufacturers whose capability fits, then coordinates sampling and production through one point of contact. It saves weeks of searching and vetting.

7. Order pooling platforms

If your quantity is below a factory's minimum, pooling platforms combine your order with other buyers. You get access to factories that would otherwise decline your order.
