Step 2: Pick the right GSM

Our fabric guide covers GSM for every product.

GSM (grams per square metre) measures fabric weight. Heavier fabric feels more premium and holds structure; lighter fabric is cooler.

  • 160 to 180 GSM: light everyday tees, summer wear, merchandise.
  • 180 to 220 GSM: mid-weight regular-fit tees with a quality feel.
  • 220 to 260+ GSM: heavyweight oversized streetwear tees with a structured drape.

Step 3: Decide the fit

  • Regular: classic fit, works for most customers.
  • Oversized: dropped shoulders, wider body and longer sleeves. Popular in streetwear.
  • Boxy: wide and slightly cropped.
  • Slim: closer to the body, often with some stretch.

Write measurements for every size in a tech pack. Our tech pack guide shows the key points to measure, and the t-shirt tech pack example shows a complete size chart.

What drives t-shirt cost

  • Fabric and GSM: heavier and combed or bio-washed cotton costs more.
  • Quantity: setup costs spread over more pieces means a lower unit price. This is why pooling helps.
  • Colours: custom dyed colours can carry dye-lot minimums.
  • Decoration: number of print colours, print size and placements.
  • Branding: custom neck labels, hang tags and packaging.
