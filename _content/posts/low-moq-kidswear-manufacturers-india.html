Short answerTo start a kidswear brand with low MOQ in India, focus on one age band, use soft breathable cotton fabrics, build safety into the design (no neck drawcords, secure fastenings, soft labels), and source from a kidswear hub like Tiruppur. Spread a small order across a focused size set, or join a pooled lot to order fewer pieces at near-bulk pricing.

Parents buy kidswear again and again as children grow, which makes it a strong category for a new brand. The catch is that one style needs many sizes, and parents expect comfort and safety without compromise. Both affect how you plan a small first order.

Pick a focused age band

Age bandTypical productsWhat matters most
0 to 24 monthsRompers, onesies, jhablas, setsSoftness, easy changing, gentle fastenings
2 to 8 yearsTees, shorts, co-ords, dresses, nightwearDurability, washability, fun prints
8 to 14 yearsCasual and occasion wearStyle that feels grown-up, fit

Starting with one band keeps sizes, patterns and stock under control.

Fabrics that work for kids

  • Cotton single jersey: soft and breathable for tees and dresses.
  • Interlock: thicker, smoother and stable, popular for baby wear.
  • Rib: stretchy, good for fitted pieces and leggings.
  • Organic cotton: a strong selling point with parents, at a higher fabric cost.
  • Muslin and cotton voile: light wovens for summer and swaddles.

More detail in our fabric guide.

Safety and comfort checklist

  • No drawcords at the hood or neck for young children.
  • Buttons, snaps and trims attached firmly and pull-tested.
  • Flat or covered seams near the skin, with no scratchy edges.
  • Soft labels, or printed labels instead of stiff woven tags.
  • Skin-safe dyes and print inks, and no small decorations that can come loose.
  • Easy openings for babies, such as envelope necks and snap crotches.

Check the labelling and safety rules that apply to children's products where you sell, including on marketplaces.

Where kidswear is made in India

Tiruppur is the largest hub for cotton knit kidswear and baby wear. Kolkata makes value kidswear and hosiery. Delhi NCR and Mumbai have units for occasion wear and woven pieces. See our guide to India's apparel hubs.

Planning a small order across sizes

A style in five sizes needs a size split. Weight it towards the sizes that sell best rather than ordering equal amounts. For example, a 100-piece order of a toddler tee might be split 15, 25, 25, 20 and 15 across five sizes. Track sell-through by size and adjust your next order.

How to order kidswear in small batches

  1. Choose your age band and 2 to 4 hero styles.
  2. Specify fabric, GSM, colours, prints and safety details in a tech pack.
  3. Approve a sample in at least two sizes, and wash-test it.
  4. Order through a pooled lot or a small-batch kidswear unit.
  5. Inspect fastenings and seams on arrival before selling.
