How the calculator works

It follows the same method as our guide on how to price your clothing products:

  1. Landed cost per piece = fabric and making + printing + labels and trims + packaging + (freight + sampling) ÷ order quantity.
  2. Selling price before GST = landed cost × your markup.
  3. Suggested MRP = selling price + GST, rounded up to a price ending in 49 or 99.
  4. Cost to sell one order = shipping + fees (as a % of MRP) + return rate × cost per return + marketing per order.
  5. Profit per order = selling price before GST − landed cost − cost to sell one order.

If you already have a price in mind, type it into Or test your own MRP and the calculator shows the profit at that price instead.

What to put in each field

  • Fabric and making: the factory's per-piece price for the garment. If you buy fabric yourself, add fabric cost per piece and the CMT (cut, make, trim) charge.
  • Printing or embroidery: per piece, at your quantity. Small runs cost more per piece; see screen printing vs DTF vs DTG.
  • One-off costs: anything you pay once per order, like freight, sampling and tech pack work. The calculator spreads them over your quantity.
  • Cost per return: reverse shipping plus the forward shipping you lose and the cost of repacking.
  • Marketing per order: your monthly ad and creator spend divided by monthly orders.

Ways to improve the numbers

  • Lower landed cost without cutting quality: stick to standard fabrics and trims, and avoid below-MOQ surcharges by pooling your order on Smallotz.
  • Cut returns: an accurate size chart and honest photos reduce size-related returns.
  • Push prepaid orders: cash-on-delivery adds charges and cancellations.
  • Price per channel: marketplaces take commission and see more returns, so they usually need a higher multiple than your own site.
